## Design Specificity Verdict

**Partly specific — and thinner than it looks.** An unrelated Gleam/BEAM library could adopt this site by editing ~30 hex values and two image files, and nothing structural would betray the swap. `custom.css` is 87 lines, 21 of which are `--sl-color-*` overrides; the rest styles two small splash elements. There is no authored component, no "Card Catalog" motif from DESIGN.md §1, no signature layout detail. The North Star metaphor exists in the design doc and nowhere in the DOM.

The one genuine exception is `berry-code-themes.mjs`. It solves a hard constraint — one hue family, no color-coding available — by encoding emphasis in lightness and voice in chroma, with contrast verified inline per token. Code blocks are the most-read component here and they look deliberately made. That file could not be lifted onto another project unchanged.

Where it decays into "sterile default Starlight": mermaid diagrams render 100% vendor-default (`fill:#333`, `"trebuchet ms"`, `#ECECFF`/`#9370DB` nodes, `#FFFFDE`/`#AAAA33` subgraphs) on the two pages that explain the mechanism; asides use stock `hsl(41,90%,60%)` orange and `hsl(234,90%,60%)` blue; the 404 is stock with no links.

**Deterministic scan**: `detect.mjs --json website/src` returned **0 findings, exit 0** — verified real (re-run with `--no-config`, no ignore rules in `.impeccable/config.local.json`). But the clean result is much narrower than it appears: `.md` and `.mdx` are not in `SCANNABLE_EXTENSIONS`, so only 4 files were actually read (`custom.css`, `fonts.css`, `Head.astro`, `content.config.ts`). Thirteen content files were never scanned. URL-mode scan was unavailable (puppeteer not installed).

**Browser overlay** (injected successfully on 4 pages via live-server on :8401, since stopped): 1 finding on `/`, 3 on `/quick-start/`, 14 on `/guides/write-modes/`, 38 on `/advanced/persistence-operations/` — dominated by `line-length` (~90 chars/line) and, on the persistence page, six each of `cramped-padding` on mermaid `<g>`, `.labelBkg`, and `.edgeLabel`, plus `shape-assembled-illustration` (20 primitives, 8 fill colors). The mermaid findings are the deterministic echo of the design review's P0.

**Where the detector caught what the review missed**: box-shadows. DESIGN.md:111/127 says "Never add a box-shadow to create hierarchy on cards, asides, or navigation" and "Shadow Strategy: None," yet 9 visible shadowed elements were measured on `/guides/write-modes/` alone — every code block (`rgba(0,0,0,0.157) 1.6px 1.6px 3.2px`, and `0.357` alpha on `/quick-start/`), both pagination links, the mobile menu button, and the theme dropdown. All pure black, zero berry tint. Only the search dialog is exempt.

**Where the detector had a blind spot**: its No-Gray audit returned PASS, but it enumerated `color`/`background-color`/`border-*` only. SVG uses `fill`, so the mermaid `#333` text and `#9370DB`/`#AAAA33` strokes were never in scope. The design review's finding stands; the PASS does not.

**False positives**: skip-link `#0000ee`/`#9e9eff` (UA default on the visually-hidden state; focuses to `#ffffff` on `#8f195a` at 8.56:1), bare `<pre>` at `font-family: monospace` (holds no text nodes — the child `<code>` sets Spline Sans Mono), and a self-corrected "12 elements wider than viewport" that was a naive width check inside `overflow-x:auto` containers.

## Overall Impression

The writing is better than the design. `persistence-operations.md`'s Durability story, `limitations.md`'s `ShelfError` table, and the four-line alternatives comparison on the splash are the work of a maintainer who respects the reader — honest about `fsync(2)`, honest about 2 GB, honest about where Mnesia wins. That is PRODUCT.md's "honest about trade-offs" principle actually executed, and it is the reason this site earns trust.

What undercuts it is that the design system was committed in DESIGN.md and then implemented only where CSS variables reach. Colors and type were themed. Diagrams, asides, and elevation were not — and those are exactly the surfaces a BEAM developer reads as *maintained or abandoned*. The single biggest opportunity: finish theming the component layer. Mermaid config and aside color tokens are perhaps 60 lines of work that would close most of the gap between "themed Starlight" and "authored site."

## What's Working

1. **`berry-code-themes.mjs` is real design work, not a swap.** Tokens differentiated on lightness (emphasis) and chroma (voice) instead of hue, with every ratio documented inline — `8.58:1`, `6.50:1`, `13.37:1`. Measured live: code text hits 16.77:1 light and 15.69:1 dark. Function names read as the brightest thing in the block; comments recede without dropping below AA.
2. **The Durability story is model information design.** A 5-layer table locating each call, then a "You want… / Then call" table inverting it for task-driven readers, then a plain statement of what shelf *cannot* do and what to do instead. It declares itself canonical and `write-modes.md:78` and `troubleshooting.md:63` link to it rather than restating it.
3. **Typography discipline holds under measurement.** 16px/1.65 body, 650 headings, `text-wrap: balance` on headings and `pretty` on paragraphs, 70ch cap on the splash only. Body contrast measures 12.58:1 light and 9.93:1 dark. Every contrast pair tested across both themes passed — 30+ measurements, zero failures.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Mermaid diagrams render in vendor default styling
**Why it matters**: The injected SVG stylesheet is `font-family:"trebuchet ms",verdana,arial; fill:#333`, with `#ECECFF`/`#9370DB` nodes and `#FFFFDE`/`#AAAA33` subgraphs. That breaks three of DESIGN.md's five named rules in one element — No-Gray, One Family, Two-Ends — and it appears on `/introduction/` (the first explanatory page an evaluator reads) and `/advanced/persistence-operations/` (where they check crash semantics). In dark theme it is a bright foreign rectangle mid-page. A developer deciding whether this library is maintained reads that as *thrown together* — PRODUCT.md's third anti-reference verbatim. The overlay independently flagged 18 `cramped-padding` findings inside the same diagrams.
**Fix**: `astro.config.mjs:17` calls bare `mermaid()` with no configuration. Replace with `mermaid({ theme: 'base', autoTheme: true, mermaidConfig: { fontFamily: '"Spline Sans Variable", sans-serif', themeVariables: {…} } })`, supplying two variable sets from the same ramp `berry-code-themes.mjs` already uses (dark: `#4d0022` surfaces, `#f8aabe` text, `#ff729e` lines; light: `#fff3f5` surfaces, `#6b0031` text, `#a2004e` lines). Also reverse the subgraph order in `persistence-operations.md:140-154` — mermaid renders `wt` above `wb`, contradicting every other page's WriteBack-first ordering.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

### [P1] The component layer was never themed — asides and elevation both defect
**Why it matters**: Two failures with one root cause. Asides use Starlight stock hues: the `:::caution[Data loss on crash]` on `write-modes.md:29-31` renders mustard `#73520d` on `#fceac5`, and the `:::note` blocks on all three table guides render in `hsl(234,…)` **blue** — the exact blue-tint DESIGN.md §2 names as an instant tell. Asides carry the two things an evaluator is most anxious about — data loss and API asymmetries — and they currently look pasted in from another site.
**Fix**: In `custom.css`, add per-theme overrides for `--sl-color-orange{,-low,-high}`, `--sl-color-blue{,-low,-high}`, `--sl-color-purple{,-low,-high}`, `--sl-color-green{,-low,-high}`, `--sl-color-red{,-low,-high}`, mapping caution/note/tip/danger onto ramp steps that hold ≥4.5:1 in both themes; differentiate the four types by ramp position and border weight rather than hue. Then null the shadows on `figure.frame`, `a.astro-crevedg2` (pagination), the mobile menu button, and `.dropdown`, replacing them with a one-step tonal shift plus a hairline in the mid-ramp. Leave the search dialog alone.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`
